# FRC Robot CAD

A custom FRC-style robot designed in Onshape. The goal is to design a complete robot from the ground up, including the drivebase, swerve modules, intake, indexer, and structural frame.

## Drivebase

![Drivebase](https://halflife.hackclub-assets.com/hackclub-half-life/sessions/qS2bceLRVTmEuNM9IZhPK3Fw79ZUnkMt/4b38b21789b437e42f17e2a8d4bd2d49821aa0cecf4875aeba3691e794d9384f.png)

The drivebase features a custom frame, bumpers, structural supports, and space for the battery and electronics. The design uses four SDS MK5N swerve modules.

## Swerve Modules

![MK5N Swerve Modules](https://halflife.hackclub-assets.com/hackclub-half-life/sessions/qS2bceLRVTmEuNM9IZhPK3Fw79ZUnkMt/77bd19ca6413a849c65c7ee01bb6151a7aa1bd6b88b757057b2757bbaa440398.png)

The drivetrain was initially designed around MK4i modules before being updated to the newer MK5N design. Reference CAD models from SDS helped with fitting the modules into the frame.

## Indexer

![Indexer](https://halflife.hackclub-assets.com/hackclub-half-life/sessions/qS2bceLRVTmEuNM9IZhPK3Fw79ZUnkMt/3095c7bc96a08ad34c8d5d11e70afc7b87a074697fd13cf033ef46021b258197.png)

The indexer is currently being developed. I'm refining the roller and end cap dimensions to make sure the parts fit together and align properly with the drivebase.

## Intake

![Intake](https://halflife.hackclub-assets.com/hackclub-half-life/sessions/qS2bceLRVTmEuNM9IZhPK3Fw79ZUnkMt/5a13928362df5ace7874a0c9b3f73da0d4a7c4e0195e419158066062e6ba1545.png)

The intake started as a basic dimensional prototype and is now being developed into a more complete design. The next step is to refine its dimensions and integrate it with the rest of the robot.

## Current Progress

The drivebase is nearly complete, and work is continuing on the intake, indexer, and electronics layout. The full robot assembly is still a work in progress, with more refinement and integration ahead.

**Tools:** Onshape  
**Project type:** Mechanical design and CAD
